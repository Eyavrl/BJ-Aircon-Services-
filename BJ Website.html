B&J Aircon Maintenance & Services was founded with one simple goal: bring professional, honest, and affordable aircon solutions to every Filipino home and business. What started as a small service operation has grown into a fully authorized dealer and service center serving the community with pride.

Over 4 years, we have built our reputation on technical mastery, transparent pricing, and a genuine commitment to customer satisfaction. Our team of certified technicians handles everything from routine cleaning to complex system repairs and brand-new installations.

YEAR 1
B&J Aircon founded — began offering residential aircon cleaning and repair services in the local community.
YEAR 2
Expanded to commercial clients and secured our first Authorized Service Center partnership with major brands.
YEAR 3
Became an Authorized Dealer — offering brand-new unit sales alongside full installation and after-sales service.
YEAR 4+
Serving hundreds of satisfied clients with a growing team of licensed technicians and expanded service coverage.
